What is Redstone Labs?

Redstone Labs is an independent software studio that builds and ships products across different markets. Instead of betting everything on one product, the studio goes where the opportunity is — designing, building, and launching real software quickly, then letting the work speak for itself.

What has Redstone Labs built?

So far Redstone Labs has shipped two products: SkillForge, a registry for AI agent skills, and Flylio, a flight search and booking interface built on Duffel's flight infrastructure. Both were designed and built in-house, going from concept to live, working products.

How can I contact Redstone Labs?

For partnerships, investment, press, or anything else, email redstonelabssupport@gmail.com or use the contact page. Every message is read by the studio, and partnership and investment inquiries get a personal reply within a few business days.
